Privacy Policy

Snake · Neon — by Aetos Systems. Last updated: 8 August 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Snake · Neon ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. You can play the app as a guest without providing any personal information. Some features, such as saving your progress and appearing on the leaderboard, require creating an account.

Information We Collect

When you choose to create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used to create and secure your account.
  • Display name — shown on your profile and the leaderboard.
  • Account identifier — a unique ID that links your data to your account.
  • Gameplay data — such as your scores, level, and badges, used for progress and the leaderboard.
  • Profile photo — only if you choose to upload one.

If you play as a guest, this information is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide app functionality: to save your progress, display your profile, and operate the global leaderboard. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Data Storage

Account data is stored securely using Supabase, our backend and database provider. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your display name, profile photo, and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboard by choice. You can control what is shown to others in the app's privacy settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive or store your payment card details.

Your Rights

You can edit your display name and privacy settings in the app, and sign out at any time. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

The app is rated for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the optional account details described above.

Contact
